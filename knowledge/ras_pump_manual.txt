RAS Pump XP-500 Troubleshooting Guide

SYMPTOM: Elevated motor amperage.
Potential causes include:
1. Pump obstruction or ragging at the suction or impeller.
2. Increased discharge head or downstream restriction.
3. Bearing degradation or inadequate lubrication.
4. Coupling misalignment.
5. Impeller damage or excessive mechanical drag.

SYMPTOM: Rattling or abnormal vibration.
Recommended checks:
1. Inspect bearings for elevated temperature, noise, or looseness.
2. Verify coupling alignment and fastener torque.
3. Inspect suction and impeller for debris.
4. Check vibration trend against normal baseline.
5. Verify pump is not operating far from its normal hydraulic range.

CAUTION:
Isolate and lock out equipment before intrusive inspection. Follow facility lockout/tagout procedures and OEM safety guidance.

ESCALATION:
If bearing temperature exceeds 180 F, vibration exceeds 0.50 in/s, or motor current remains above 120 percent of rated current, remove the unit from service if operational redundancy allows and escalate to maintenance supervision.
